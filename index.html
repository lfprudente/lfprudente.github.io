I work on continuous optimization, mainly on methods for multiobjective and vector optimization.

Other interests include nonlinear programming, augmented Lagrangian methods and numerical linear algebra for optimization. Code for most papers is on GitHub.

B.Sc. in Mathematics (2006), M.Sc. (2009) and Ph.D. (2012) in Applied Mathematics from IMECC, University of Campinas (UNICAMP).
